# Brilliant Mind Travels & Tours

**Your Trusted Partner For Global Travel And Opportunities**

Official website: [www.brilliantmindtravels.com](https://www.brilliantmindtravels.com/)

Brilliant Mind Travels & Tours is a travel and immigration consultancy helping individuals achieve their travel, study abroad, and visa goals with professional guidance.

## Office

Olayia's Complex, beside Eyiowu Awi Pharmacy, Ede 102213, Osun State, Nigeria

## Services

- Visa assistance (student, tourist, work, business)
- Study abroad consultancy
- Tour packages
- Flight booking assistance
- Hotel reservations
- Travel consultation

## Website Features

**Public site**
- Homepage with hero, about, services, study abroad, tour packages, success stories, reviews, and latest news
- Services, Study Abroad Countries, Tour Packages, Blog/News, Testimonials, Reviews, and Contact pages
- Consultation/appointment request form
- WhatsApp integration (floating button plus service, tour, and consultation inquiry buttons that prefill the selected service or package)
- Google Maps office location
- SEO-friendly, mobile responsive, accessible, and fast loading

**Admin dashboard (secure)**
- Overview of inquiries, messages, appointments, and content counts
- Create, edit, delete, and publish/unpublish services, countries, universities, tour packages, and blog posts
- Manage testimonials (video and image) and approve or delete reviews
- View appointment requests, inquiries, and contact messages
- Media library for images, videos, and documents

## Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS
- **Backend:** Supabase (authentication, database, storage)

## Database Tables

`users`, `profiles`, `services`, `countries`, `universities`, `tour_packages`, `blog_posts`, `testimonials`, `reviews`, `appointments`, `inquiries`, `contact_messages`, `media_files`, `settings`

## Payments

This is not an online payment platform. Customers contact the company via WhatsApp, phone, or the office, and pay physically once an agreement is reached.
